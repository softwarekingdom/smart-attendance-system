Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Smart Attendance System handles information relating to institutions, teachers, students and parents when the relevant features are implemented.

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Contents

1. Introduction

Smart Attendance System is intended to help private educational institutions and tuition or training centres manage student attendance and related administrative tasks.

Depending on the features provided, the system may allow an authorised teacher to manage an institution account, student records, attendance information, parent contact details and student identification cards.

This policy describes the intended privacy practices of the system. Actual practices must match the features, services and technical safeguards that are implemented.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features used, the system may process the following categories of information:

2.1 Teacher and institution information

  • Institution or training centre name.
  • Teacher or authorised administrator's name.
  • Email address and contact number.
  • Institution address, if provided.
  • Account credentials and account-related records.

2.2 Student information

  • Student name and assigned student ID.
  • Class, course or batch details.
  • Attendance dates and attendance status.
  • Student photograph, if required for an ID card.

2.3 Parent or guardian information

  • Parent or guardian name, if provided.
  • Contact number used for attendance notifications.
  • Information necessary to prepare a relevant message.

2.4 Technical information

If implemented, technical logs may include login activity, timestamps, error information and basic security events. The system should collect only the information necessary for its intended functions.

The categories above describe intended functionality. They do not mean that every category is currently collected or stored.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing institution accounts.
  • Adding and managing student records.
  • Recording and reviewing student attendance.
  • Preparing absence notifications for parents or guardians.
  • Generating student ID cards.
  • Maintaining attendance reports for authorised users.
  • Protecting accounts and investigating technical problems.

Information should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis or other required permission.

4. Attendance Records

Attendance records may contain a student's name, assigned ID, class or course, attendance date and attendance status.

  • Attendance information should be accessible only to authorised people who need it for educational administration.
  • Teachers should take reasonable care to record attendance accurately and correct mistakes promptly.
  • Attendance information should not be publicly displayed or disclosed to unrelated people.
  • Institutions should explain their attendance-record practices to students and parents where appropriate.

5. WhatsApp Notifications

The system is intended to help a teacher prepare a WhatsApp message when a student is marked absent.

  • The teacher should review the attendance status and message before sending it.
  • Messages must not be sent automatically without the intended teacher review in the planned workflow.
  • Parent or guardian contact details should be checked for accuracy and used only for appropriate purposes.
  • The message should include only the information needed to notify the parent or guardian.

WhatsApp is a third-party service. If a teacher sends a message through WhatsApp, that service's own terms and privacy practices may apply. We cannot promise how WhatsApp processes messages or metadata.

Whether messages are sent through WhatsApp directly, through a supported integration or through a prepared message link will depend on the implementation.

6. Student ID Cards

The system may support generating multiple student ID cards for an institution.

  • Only necessary details should appear on each card.
  • Student photographs and identifiers should be handled carefully.
  • Generated cards should be shared or printed only by authorised institution personnel.
  • Institutions should protect printed cards and exported files from unauthorised access.

7. Data Sharing

Personal information should not be sold. It should only be disclosed where necessary for a legitimate system function, authorised by the responsible institution, or required or permitted by applicable law.

Depending on the eventual technical design, service providers such as hosting, database, email or messaging providers may process some information to operate the service.

The identity of these providers, the data they receive and any international transfers must be assessed and described accurately before the service launches.

8. Data Security

Appropriate safeguards should be implemented to help protect information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, loss or misuse.

Planned safeguards should include, where implemented:

  • Secure password hashing rather than plain-text storage.
  • Access controls for institution and student records.
  • Secure connections using HTTPS.
  • Server-side validation of submitted information.
  • Protection of sensitive configuration and secret keys.
  • Appropriate backups, monitoring and security updates.

No online system can guarantee absolute security. The safeguards actually available will depend on the implemented system and its hosting environment.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be kept only for as long as necessary for the stated purposes and applicable legal or institutional recordkeeping requirements.

A final retention schedule, deletion process and account closure procedure must be established before launch. Deleting an account should not automatically be represented as deleting every backup or record unless that is verified.

10. Privacy Rights and Requests

Subject to applicable law, individuals may be able to request access to their personal information, correction of inaccurate details, deletion where appropriate, or information about how their data is used.

Students or parents should contact the responsible institution or its authorised representative to raise concerns about student records.

A process for verifying requests and responding securely must be established before these rights can be handled through the system.

11. Student Information and Children

The system may process information about school-age students and other minors. Such information deserves particular care.

  • Institutions should ensure they have an appropriate legal basis and obtain any required parent or guardian permissions before providing student information.
  • Student information should not be used for advertising or unrelated profiling.
  • Access to student photographs, parent contact numbers and attendance records should be limited.
  • Student information should not be made publicly searchable or visible by default.

The system's final procedures must comply with applicable Sri Lankan law and any requirements relevant to the institution and the students it serves.

12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The current frontend does not establish a complete authentication or tracking system. If cookies, session storage, analytics or similar technologies are introduced, the policy must explain their purposes and relevant choices.

Essential authentication cookies, if used, should be configured with appropriate security protections.

13. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when system features, service providers, legal requirements or data-handling practices change.

The published policy should show its latest revision date. Where required, users should be informed of significant changes before they take effect.

14. Contact and Privacy Concerns

For questions about this policy or requests concerning personal information, please contact the service operator through the official contact channel provided by Smart Attendance System.
